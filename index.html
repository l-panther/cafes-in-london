Featured Cafes

London is one of the most vibrant cities in England, celebrated for its cultural diversity and the endless variety of things to see and do. With such a wide selection of food available across the city, finding a great place to eat can sometimes feel overwhelming. Whether you are vegetarian or a meat lover, discovering the perfect café can be a challenge.

To make your search easier, we have carefully selected four of the best cafés in London, offering something for everyone. From cosy neighbourhood favourites to well-known city icons, these cafés stand out for their quality, atmosphere, and great food.

Each location has been chosen for its unique character, welcoming environment, and consistently positive customer reviews, ensuring that you can enjoy a satisfying and memorable dining experience wherever you choose to go.

Hard Rock Cafe

★★★★☆ 102 ratings
150 Old Park Ln, W1K
Meat & Vegetarian

The Hard Rock Cafe is located on Old Park Lane since 1941, where it was the first cafe where the classes mingled. The food has American favorites like the classic burgers and salads made from the freshest ingredients.

Gallery Cafe

★★★★☆ 6 ratings
21 Old Ford Rd, E2
Vegetarian

The Gallery Cafe has a long history which dates back to 1888. This history led to the full purchase of numbers 15 and 17 Old Ford Road in 1999. A vegan and vegetarian menu, exhibitions and live events every month.

Regency Cafe

★★★☆☆ 17 ratings
17-19 Regency St, SW1P
Meat

The Regency Cafe first opened its doors in 1946 on Regency Street. This popular cafe has previously been voted the fifth best place to eat in London on Yelp in 2013. The cafe design is an art deco style.

The Table Cafe

★★★★☆ 43 ratings
20 Old St, EC1
Vegetarian

The Table Cafe is located in Southwark and is said to be one of South Bank's hidden gems. It has a groundbreaking record for the homeless charity St Mungo's. The Table Cafe is committed to sourcing quality ingredients.
